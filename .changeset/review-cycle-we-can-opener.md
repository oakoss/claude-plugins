A message that is only "we can" and a step now asks for that step, as "ok, we can" already did: "We can merge 91" grants the merge of #91, and "We can merge it when it's ready" grants auto-merge. A "we can" with anything beside it in the message, another sentence, line, clause, "and" or "then", still asks for nothing, since that is how options are listed ("We can wait. We can merge 91.", "We can push it, but I'd rather wait"). Neither does "We could merge 91".
