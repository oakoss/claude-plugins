The gate now reads your requests exactly, from a closed set, in place of the grammar that tried to read free English. A request is one of these, alone or two joined by "and" or "then", with an optional "ok," or "yes," before it and "please", "lets", "go ahead and", "you can" or "we can" leading it: commit, push, ship, open a PR, merge (it, or pull requests by number, `name#53` or link), approve, release; push to a named branch, force push with or without a lease, push a named tag, delete the branch; force push the stack, merge the stack, merge up to a pull request; finish the rebase, merge, cherry-pick or revert; reply to the review; ship, push, merge or release "them" or "both"; and the standing asks, "merge it when it's ready" or "once CI passes", and "release when the release PR is ready". A bare "yes" counts only when the agent's answer ended with a question the offer tool writes, such as "Merge #316?", in a paragraph of its own. Anything else grants nothing, and the refusal hands the agent that question, so the cost is one "yes".

What you do differently: a request chained to other work ("file the bead then ship", "merge 316 then start on the next one") now gets the question, as do "merge the release", "release 289", "mark it ready for review", a request phrased as a question ("can we merge 347?"), picks such as "merge #3 but not #4", and a yes to a question the agent wrote itself. A message with a hold word ("don't", "not yet", "wait", "stop", "later") grants nothing at all now, so "push it; don't open a PR yet" pushes nothing and holds the push. Declines are read generously where requests are read exactly: any sentence or clause that opens with one ("wait a sec", "sorry, please stop", "nvm", "I agree, but wait") takes back what a running turn was granted and ends a standing ask or an epic job, since a decline read by mistake costs one question and one missed leaves a push running. Mid-sentence ("I don't think that's right") a hold word takes nothing back. On the same 7,422 messages that rule read 5 as a decline naming no step, 3 more than the grammar. Nothing joins after a standing ask but a release: "merge it when it's ready, then release it" still releases once the version pull request is ready, while "merge it when it's ready and push it" grants nothing.

Measured on 7,422 messages typed since 2026-10-01: of the 1,440 the grammar granted, the exact reader gives the same grant field for field on 1,322 (91.8%), a plain push where the grammar took a force from the offer on 1, and nothing on 117 (8.1%), 63 of them chained to other work; it grants nothing the grammar did not. On the 304-row corpus it grants no step a row does not allow. A step named without being asked for still holds it, as before ("anything else before we ship?"); on those messages the reader holds the same step as the grammar on all but 3 that the grammar held, and holds 120 the grammar did not, nearly all of them the requests it no longer grants.

`pnpm consent:diff` and the grammar's spec are gone with the grammar, and the corpus rows lose their `missed` flag: the corpus spec checks only that no row grants a step outside its `may`.
