A yes to an offer that sets a force push against a plain push grants neither in three more phrasings: a ref with a dot in it (`fix/x.y`), a plain side written as a backticked `git push` with no flags ("or run `git push origin fix/x`?"), and a plain push asked as a follow-up question ("Force-push now? Or a normal push?"). The refusal now quotes the question that offered the choice, where a dotted ref could make it quote a later sentence.
